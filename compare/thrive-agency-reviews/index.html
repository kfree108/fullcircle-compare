Thrive Agency Reviews: Reading Them for Amazon Specifically

Thrive Internet Marketing Agency rates 4.6 out of 5 across 109 Clutch reviews and has operated since 2005. Amazon is one service inside a full digital marketing agency. That breadth is simultaneously the best reason to hire them and the reason to look elsewhere.

Disclosure first

Full Circle is an Amazon agency and competes with Thrive on Amazon briefs. Everything below is drawn from Thrive's own pages or from Clutch, and the sources are listed at the bottom.

Thrive is a different kind of company from us and from the other agencies usually compared alongside them, which is the single most important thing to understand before reading their reviews.

Who Thrive is

Thrive Internet Marketing Agency has been operating since 2005, headquartered in Arlington, Texas, with an employee band of 50 to 249 on Clutch and location pages across roughly 25 cities. It is a full digital marketing agency: SEO, PPC, social, web design, conversion rate optimisation, content, video, email — with Amazon as one service line among them.

Their Amazon offering is not thin. Their own page lists Amazon SEO, sponsored ads across Products, Brands, Display and video, DSP management, brand store design, listing optimisation, catalogue organisation, brand registry, suspension appeal, seller consulting, Amazon Posts, Creator Connections campaign management, and Amazon Attribution alongside the Brand Referral Bonus programme. They also run a reporting dashboard, Thrive Stats.

How to read a 4.6 across 109 reviews

The headline is solid: 4.6 out of 5 across 109 Clutch reviews, with a listed hourly range of $100 to $149 and a $1,000+ minimum project size. That volume of review is meaningfully more than most Amazon specialists carry.

The caveat matters just as much. Those 109 reviews span every service Thrive sells. A five-star review of a website rebuild or a local SEO campaign tells you almost nothing about how they will run a sponsored-ads account. If you are evaluating them for Amazon, the rating is close to noise on its own — ask for Amazon-only references and read those.

The consistent themes across the reviews are communication, responsiveness and on-time delivery, with named account managers coming in for repeated praise. The consistent criticism is depth of industry-specific knowledge, with more than one reviewer describing having to supply a great deal of context themselves. There are also a small number of strongly negative reviews on the public platforms, as there are for most agencies at that review volume. Read them and judge whether the specifics would apply to your engagement.

Where Thrive is genuinely the better choice

  • Amazon is one of several channels. If you also need Google, Meta, email and a website, one agency across all of it removes a coordination problem that is genuinely expensive to solve with four vendors.
  • External traffic matters to you. Their Amazon page covers Attribution and the Brand Referral Bonus, and they run the off-Amazon channels that feed them. Most Amazon-only agencies can advise on this and cannot execute it.
  • Contract flexibility. Their Amazon page states there are no long-term commitments. Worth confirming in the contract, but it is stated plainly on their own site, which is more than most.
  • Longevity. Twenty years in business is a real signal in a category where agencies appear and vanish inside three.

Where they are the wrong pick

If Amazon is the majority of your revenue, the calculus changes. Two things are visibly absent from their Amazon services page: inventory and supply chain work, and the catalogue troubleshooting depth — broken variations, stranded inventory, GS1 and UPC problems — that eats most of the operational hours on a large Amazon account. That is a scope observation from their own page, not a judgement about quality.

The second issue is attention share. On a multi-service agency, Amazon competes internally with every other line for senior time. On an Amazon-only agency it does not. Neither structure is better in the abstract; it depends entirely on how much of your business Amazon actually is.

Full Circle is a full-service Amazon management company: advertising, listings, catalogue, supply chain and the operational work that decides whether advertising can even function. $500M+ in managed spend across 100+ brands, with publicly named work including HexClad, Ridge, BK Beauty, Beardbrand, Epic Gardening and The Woobles. If Amazon is the business, hire people who only do Amazon — us or one of the specialists. Common questions

Is Thrive Agency good for Amazon specifically?

Their Amazon service list is genuinely broad — SEO, sponsored ads, DSP, brand stores, Attribution and Creator Connections. The limitation is that their 4.6 Clutch rating spans all services, so it is weak evidence about Amazon work in particular. Ask for Amazon-only client references before deciding.

How much does Thrive Agency cost?

No prices are published on their Amazon page, which describes "transparent, affordable pricing" and "no long-term contracts" without stating figures. Clutch lists a $100 to $149 hourly range and a $1,000+ minimum project size, both self-reported. Ask for the fee structure and what triggers an increase.

What do the critical reviews say?

The recurring theme is industry-specific depth, with reviewers describing having to provide substantial context about their own sector. At 109 reviews there are also a small number of strongly negative accounts, which is normal at that volume across every agency. Read the low-star reviews directly and judge relevance to your own brief.

Thrive or an Amazon-only agency?

Decide on revenue share. If Amazon is a minority of sales and you need several channels handled, one multi-channel agency is a genuinely better structure. If Amazon is the majority of your revenue, hire people who only do Amazon, because catalogue, case and supply work is where the hours go and it is specialist by nature.
