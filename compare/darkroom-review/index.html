Disclosure before anything else

Full Circle competes with Darkroom for Amazon work. A rival's review of a rival is worth reading only if you can check it, so every factual claim below comes from Darkroom's own published pages or from a public profile you can open yourself, and the sources are listed at the foot of this page.

We have not gone looking for a weakness to plant. On several of the briefs described further down we would tell a brand to talk to Darkroom rather than to us, and mean it.

Who Darkroom is, on their own pages

Darkroom Digital operates from hubs in New York, Lisbon and São Paulo, and the current site describes the firm as an AI-native advertising agency "entrusted with commerce innovation by companies like Adobe, Amazon, and Everlane". Their homepage states that they operate as one integrated team managing over $250M in media across channels, and they were named an Inc. 5000 honoree in 2025.

The service list is broad and unusually specific about which parts are separate disciplines: paid media management, performance creative, TikTok Shop management, Amazon management, retention, retail media, creator programmes, conversion rate optimisation, websites and a design studio. The Amazon page covers marketplace strategy, sponsored ads, DSP and Amazon Marketing Cloud, plus listing and storefront creative, inventory and operations, and multi-marketplace work across Walmart, TikTok Shop and Instacart.

Their case-study index names the clients rather than hiding behind categories — Everlane, Olipop, Crate&Barrel, Nécessaire, DedCool, Morphe, Public Goods, Anne Klein and others — with the Everlane entry describing a restructured Amazon presence and a 126% revenue increase in three months. Named clients with named outcomes are more accountable than anonymised case studies, and that deserves credit.

What the third-party review record does and does not show

This is the part where a competitor's page usually reaches for an insinuation. The plain facts instead.

Darkroom's Clutch profile carries firmographic details and no client reviews. We could not re-verify the firmographic figures on the live profile today, so we are not reprinting them here. A Trustpilot profile exists for the domain with no reviews on it either. Employee-side review sites carry a few dozen reviews, which describe working there rather than buying from them, and are not a client signal.

None of that is a mark against the work. Plenty of strong agencies have never run a review programme, and several of the largest names in Amazon services have similarly empty directory profiles. It does, however, change your diligence job: with no third-party corpus to read, the burden shifts entirely onto references, case-study denominators and what you can test on a call.

For symmetry: Full Circle is not on Clutch either, and you should treat our absence exactly as you treat theirs.

How to diligence an agency with few public reviews

  • Ask for two references at your size in your category — not their showcase accounts. A firm with strong retention will find two quickly.
  • Ask for the denominator behind every case-study figure. A 126% revenue increase in three months is a real claim with a real shape; what turns it into information is the starting revenue, the season, and whether anything else changed at the same time. Reputable agencies answer this without flinching.
  • Ask who did that work and whether they are still there. Case studies age; teams turn over.
  • Ask for a live read of your account on the call. Fifteen minutes, same access for every finalist. What they find is the only comparable test you can run.
  • Check the exit terms before the start terms. Notice period, ownership of creative files, whose seat any display campaigns run on, and what leaves with you.

Where Darkroom is genuinely strong

  • Creative as the engine, not a service line. Their own positioning is that creative is the biggest growth lever and short-form video is central to nearly every performance thesis. A design studio and content production sit in-house. If your ads are losing to better-looking ads, that is the correct diagnosis and they are built around it.
  • Genuinely omnichannel. Amazon, TikTok Shop, direct-to-consumer and retail media run by one team. For a consumer brand whose customer moves across all of those in a week, one integrated group beats coordinating four vendors.
  • They publish prices. A per-service rate card with a monthly starting figure for each line is close to unheard of in this category. It is the single easiest thing an agency can do to make itself comparable, and almost nobody does it.
  • Named clients, named results. Recognisable consumer brands on the case-study page, with the work described specifically.

Testing the "AI-native" claim — theirs, ours and everyone's

Darkroom's homepage leads with being the first AI-native advertising agency. Nearly every firm in this category now says something similar, including the group we belong to, so the useful move is not to argue about who said it first but to have one test you apply to all of them.

Ask what the machine decides without a human, on what cadence, and what a person checks afterwards. There are three honest answers — the software drafts and a human approves, the software acts inside limits a human set, or the software is used for analysis and humans do everything else — and all three are legitimate. What tells you something is whether the answer is that concrete.

Then ask the follow-up: is the software yours, or licensed? Both are fine. Owned software means the roadmap follows client problems and the agency can build a fix rather than request one. Licensed software means someone else carries the maintenance and the capability arrives fully formed. Ours is built in-house, which is a choice with costs as well as benefits, and we would rather you knew which trade you were buying than took the phrase on trust from anyone.

Who they suit better than us — and where we would keep the brief

Darkroom is the better call when the growth engine is creative and the customer is spread across storefronts: a consumer brand shipping short-form video weekly, selling on TikTok Shop and Amazon and its own site at once, wanting one team across all of it. It is also the better call if you specifically want an agency that will show you a price before a discovery call.

We would keep the brief where Amazon is the business and the constraint sits underneath advertising — a catalogue that will not hold its variations together, a listing suppressed mid-quarter, cases with Seller Support that keep stalling, a restock cycle costing rank every eight weeks. Which one you should actually pick

Darkroom suits consumer brands whose growth engine is short-form creative running across several storefronts at once, and brands who value seeing a price before a sales call. Full Circle suits brands where Amazon is the business and the constraint sits under advertising, in catalogue, cases or supply. The review record is thin on both sides, so judge both of us on references and on what we find in your live account.

What to do with this

Judge this on the job you actually need done, not the feature list. Pull your own search-term report for the last 90 days and total the spend against terms that produced no orders — on the account above that was 33.6% of everything spent. Then ask whether the thing you are about to buy closes that gap, or just shows it to you.

Common questions

Is Darkroom a good agency for Amazon?

On the public evidence, it is a credible one: a named Amazon service line covering sponsored ads, DSP, AMC, listings and creative, recognisable consumer brands on the case-study page, and Inc. 5000 recognition. The fit question is whether your growth lever is creative and multichannel. If Amazon is your entire business and the problem sits in catalogue or supply, a marketplace-first firm is the better shape.

Why are there no Darkroom reviews on Clutch?

Their profile exists with firmographic details but no client reviews, and the same is true of their Trustpilot listing. The most likely explanation is the ordinary one: they have never run a review-collection programme, which is true of a large share of agencies including us. Read it as an absence of data rather than as evidence, and shift your diligence to references and case-study denominators.

How much does Darkroom charge?

They publish a starting price for each service line on their own pricing page, including Amazon management, most of them monthly, and the figures sit in the range you would expect for a mid-market agency retainer. What a starting price does not tell you is scope — how many ASINs, whether creative production is inside it, whether media percentages apply above a threshold. Ask for those before comparing it with any other quote.

Darkroom or Full Circle?

Darkroom if creative is your constraint and your customer is spread across TikTok Shop, your own site and Amazon at once. Full Circle if Amazon is the business and the ceiling sits in listings, catalogue, cases or stock rather than in the ad account. If you want one function run without any agency relationship, Dr. PPC covers advertising as a product at a published price.
